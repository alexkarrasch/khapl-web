Hydrotechnical engineering for the mining and resources sector

Water clarity. Balanced decisions.

KHAPL provides water resources services for the Mining and Resources sector: Planning, Risk assessment, Hydrology, Hydraulics, Design, Closure, Modelling and GIS.

KHAPL works across Australia and can be engaged directly, as a specialist subconsultant or for independent third-party review.

Core capabilities

01

Water Balance Modelling & Accounting

Purpose-built models for forecasts, storage and transfer systems, supply reliability, mine-plan scenarios, inventory reconciliation and water accounting.

02

Site Water Management

Assessment and design of site-wide water systems, including wet-season planning, dams, transfers, diversions, monitoring, operational risk, rehabilitation and closure.

03

Waste Management

Water planning and design for waste landforms and facilities, including drainage, containment, staging, capping, rehabilitation, closure and risk assessment.

04

Hydrology

Continuous simulation and design-event hydrology, including calibrated rainfall-runoff models, arid catchments, inflow estimates, dam-break inputs and extreme events.

05

Hydraulic Analysis & Design

Hydraulic modelling and design assessment for floods, culverts, drains, spillways, levees, bunds, diversions, causeways and breach flows.

06

Climate Analysis & Climate Change

Analysis of historical climate records, statistical screening, extreme events and future climate scenarios, including effects on water systems and infrastructure to 2100.

07

Approvals & Strategic Advisory

Water-related inputs to EA, EIS, EPBC and planning approvals, including compliance advice, regulator engagement, risk studies and independent review.

08

Spatial & Digital Analysis

Mapping, GIS and terrain modelling, Python automation, data processing and client-facing model dashboards.

09

Design Model 3D Printing

Physical terrain, landform and infrastructure models for option comparison, design reviews, constructability discussions and technical communication.
